About the Mission

Four pillars of transformative outreach in Lesotho communities

Educational Support

Providing academic assistance, tutoring, and mentorship to help students unlock their potential and succeed in their educational journey.

Practical Assistance

Distributing non-perishable food, clothing, and toys to families, meeting their immediate needs with care and compassion.

Community Building

Creating lasting relationships with local communities through genuine connection, cultural exchange, and collaborative service initiatives.

Faith & Hope

Sharing the Gospel message and offering spiritual encouragement to build lasting faith in local communities.

Our Impact

From 29 November to 5 December 2026, Every Nation Campus (ENC) WITS returns to Butha-Buthe for seven days of outreach. Our purpose is to serve students and families through tutoring, mentorship, practical care, and sharing the Gospel. We are building on the relationships formed in 2025, with a heart to listen, encourage, and walk alongside the community.

01 / Practical care

Generosity, made tangible.

From 95 packs of donated clothing, every student and teacher received an outfit. Some families also received clothes for their households and grocery packs to help meet everyday needs.

02 / Learning & mentorship

Confidence beyond the classroom.

We tutored children in English and Maths and saw a change in their attitude towards their studies and their confidence in public speaking. Personal conversations offered encouragement and space to talk about treating one another with respect, including respect for the other gender.

03 / Serving the school

A fresh space for the teachers.

We painted the staffroom walls and repaired the floor, renewing a space used by the teachers who invest in these learners every day.

04 / Faith in the community

Taking the Good News into town.

On Friday, we held an open-air service in town and went through the streets sharing the Gospel and speaking with people in the community.

This would not have been possible without your partnership.
